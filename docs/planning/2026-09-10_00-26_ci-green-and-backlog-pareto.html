The 4% for 64%: regression armor. These tests are the difference between "works on my machine" and "works under 429s".
The 20% for 80%: operator value. Per-check latency in metrics + NDJSON export (F5), per-source grouping for aggregate pages (F11), and example dogfooding toggles turn the 0.7.0 UI investment into consumable observability.
